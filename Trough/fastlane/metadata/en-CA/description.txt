Log every TRT injection, see your estimated testosterone levels between doses, and keep your bloodwork organized, all in one private app.

Trough is a testosterone and injection tracker built for men on TRT and hormone therapy. Log shots in seconds, get a site-rotation suggestion for the next one, and check in daily in 30 seconds. No account, no ads.

• 42 badges to earn for logging and consistency
• Daily challenges and celebrations when you hit a milestone
• Share cards for your streak, rank and badges
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, including a next-badge widget

INJECTION TRACKER
• Log dose, compound, and injection site in seconds
• Calendar view of every shot you've logged
• Site-rotation suggestions for your next injection
• Injection-day reminders and a Live Activity countdown to your next dose
• Testosterone cypionate, enanthate, propionate, undecanoate and multi-ester protocols

ESTIMATED LEVELS (PK CURVES, PRO)
• See an estimated blood-level curve between doses, built from your own injection log
• Estimated peak, trough, and current level. These are model estimates, not lab results.

DAILY CHECK-IN + PROTOCOL SCORE
• Rate energy, mood, libido, sleep, and mental clarity in about 30 seconds
• Your answers roll up into a Protocol Score from 0 to 100, so you can see trends over time

BLOODWORK TRACKING (PRO)
• 19 markers, including total and free testosterone, estradiol (E2), hematocrit, and PSA
• Reference ranges and trend charts for each marker across lab draws

PEPTIDES, GLP-1s & ADJUNCTS (PRO)
• 34 presets for peptides, GLP-1s, and adjuncts like hCG and anastrozole
• Schedules and reminders alongside your testosterone protocol

APPLE HEALTH, EXPORT & DOCTOR VISITS

• Doctor-visit PDF report to bring to your next appointment (Pro)
